# Meridian Industries — Employee Handbook (HR Policy Reference)

Effective Date: 1 January 2026. This document is the authoritative reference for
leave, payroll, and conduct policies at Meridian Industries. It applies to all
full-time employees across all locations unless a country-specific addendum
overrides a clause below.

## 1. Leave Policy Overview

Meridian Industries provides the following leave categories to full-time
employees: Earned Leave (EL), Casual Leave (CL), Sick Leave (SL), Maternity
Leave, Paternity Leave, and Bereavement Leave. Leave accrues on the first day
of each calendar month and does not carry over between leave types.

## 2. Earned Leave (EL)

Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of Earned Leave per completed month of
service, totaling 18 days per calendar year. Earned Leave must be applied for
at least 3 working days in advance through the HR portal, except in
emergencies where retroactive approval may be granted by the reporting
manager. A maximum of 10 unused Earned Leave days may be carried forward into
the next calendar year; any balance beyond 10 days is forfeited on 31
December. Earned Leave cannot be availed during an employee's first 90 days
of employment (see Section 8, Probation Period).

## 3. Casual Leave (CL)

Employees receive 6 Casual Leave days per calendar year, credited in full at
the start of the year. Casual Leave is intended for short, unplanned personal
matters and may be applied for with as little as 1 day of advance notice.
Casual Leave cannot be combined with Earned Leave to create a leave block
longer than 5 consecutive working days without Head of Department approval.
Unused Casual Leave does not carry forward and lapses on 31 December.

## 4. Sick Leave (SL)

Employees are entitled to 12 Sick Leave days per calendar year. Sick Leave
taken for more than 2 consecutive working days requires a medical
certificate uploaded to the HR portal within 5 working days of returning to
work. Sick Leave may be applied for on the day of absence itself (same-day
notice is acceptable). Unused Sick Leave does not carry forward.

## 5. Maternity Leave

Employees who are the birthing parent are entitled to 26 weeks of fully paid
Maternity Leave for the first two children, and 12 weeks for the third child
onward. Maternity Leave may begin up to 8 weeks before the expected delivery
date. A minimum of 15 calendar days' written notice before the intended leave
start date is required, along with a physician's certificate confirming the
expected delivery date. Employees returning from Maternity Leave are
entitled to the same or an equivalent role and may request flexible working
arrangements for up to 6 months following their return.

## 6. Paternity Leave

Employees who are not the birthing parent are entitled to 2 weeks (10
working days) of fully paid Paternity Leave, to be availed within 3 months
of the child's birth or adoption date. Paternity Leave may be split into a
maximum of 2 blocks. A minimum of 7 calendar days' written notice is
required before the intended leave start date, except in cases of premature
birth.

## 7. Bereavement Leave

Employees are entitled to 5 working days of paid Bereavement Leave upon the
death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, or sibling), and
2 working days for the death of an extended family member (grandparent,
grandparent-in-law, or in-law). Bereavement Leave does not require advance
notice and may be applied for retroactively within 10 working days.

## 8. Probation Period

All new employees serve a probation period of 90 calendar days from their
date of joining, extendable by up to 30 additional days at the reporting
manager's discretion in case of performance concerns. During probation,
employees accrue Earned Leave and Casual Leave but may not avail Earned
Leave. Sick Leave and Bereavement Leave remain available during probation.
Confirmation of employment occurs automatically at the end of the probation
period unless HR issues a written extension or termination notice.

## 9. Notice Period for Leave Applications

Standard leave categories (Earned Leave, Casual Leave) require the advance
notice periods specified in their respective sections. Leave requests
submitted with insufficient notice may still be approved at the discretion
of the reporting manager but are flagged for HR review. Sick Leave,
Bereavement Leave, and emergency Casual Leave are exempt from advance notice
requirements.

## 10. Notice Period for Resignation

Employees below Manager grade must provide 30 calendar days' written notice
of resignation. Employees at Manager grade and above must provide 60
calendar days' written notice. During the notice period, any unused Earned
Leave may be adjusted against the notice period only with prior written
approval from HR; unused Earned Leave beyond what is approved for adjustment
is paid out at the employee's last drawn basic daily rate.

## 11. Payroll Cycle

Salaries are processed on a monthly cycle and disbursed on the last working
day of each month. The payroll cutoff for attendance, leave, and expense
adjustments is the 25th of each month; any changes submitted after the
cutoff are reflected in the following month's payroll cycle. Payslips are
made available on the HR portal within 2 working days of disbursement.

## 12. Payroll Deductions

Standard payroll deductions include statutory tax withholding, retirement
fund contributions (8% of basic salary, matched by a 4% employer
contribution), and health insurance premiums where applicable. Unpaid leave
(leave taken beyond an employee's available balance in any category) is
deducted at the employee's last drawn basic daily rate. Any advance salary
or loan repayments follow the schedule agreed upon at the time of
disbursement.

## 13. Payroll Correction Requests

Employees who identify a discrepancy in their payslip must raise a payroll
correction request through the HR portal within 45 calendar days of the
disbursement date. Corrections are reviewed by the Payroll team within 5
working days. Corrections exceeding 10% of an employee's monthly gross
salary require additional sign-off from the Finance Controller before
disbursement in the next cycle.

## 14. Overtime Policy

Overtime is defined as hours worked beyond 40 hours in a calendar week for
employees in overtime-eligible roles. Overtime is compensated at 1.5 times
the employee's standard hourly rate for the first 8 overtime hours in a week
and 2 times the standard hourly rate beyond that. Employees may not work
more than 12 hours of overtime in a single calendar week without written
approval from both their reporting manager and HR. Overtime must be logged
in the attendance system within 3 working days of being worked to be
eligible for compensation.

## 15. Remote Work and Attendance

Employees in eligible roles may work remotely up to 2 days per week with
reporting manager approval, logged in advance through the HR portal.
Attendance is tracked via the HR portal's daily check-in; failure to check
in for more than 3 unexplained working days in a calendar month may result
in an HR inquiry. Remote work days are not a substitute for approved leave.

## 16. Mandatory Compliance Training

All employees must complete mandatory compliance training (covering
workplace conduct, data privacy, and anti-harassment policy) within 30 days
of joining and annually thereafter by 31 March each year. Managers and above
must additionally complete People Management compliance training annually.
Failure to complete mandatory training by the deadline is escalated to the
employee's reporting manager and recorded in the compliance dashboard.

## 17. Grievance and Escalation

Employees with HR-related grievances should first raise the matter with
their reporting manager or directly with HR via the HR portal's grievance
module. Grievances must be acknowledged by HR within 3 working days and
resolved or escalated within 15 working days. Grievances involving a
conflict of interest with the reporting manager may be escalated directly to
the Head of HR.
